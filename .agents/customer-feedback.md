## Research Synthesis

### Current Confidence
**Sample:** 5 pre-launch survey responses from January 2026.

**Confidence level:** Medium for the existence of planning and expense friction; low-to-medium for specific buyer personas because the sample is small and includes mixed roles.

### 2026-05-20 Customer Research Run

**Research question:** Which business traveller or business travel-adjacent segment feels the strongest pain from planning UK multi-leg journeys, and is most likely to test EndMile now?

**Current answer:** The strongest validated pain is not "carbon reporting" on its own. It is the pre-travel comparison problem: working out a practical route when cost, timing, transfers, disruption, and expense rules all matter. Carbon remains a useful differentiator, but the early evidence suggests it should not be the only lead message.

**Evidence quality:** Directional only. The survey has 5 responses, all pre-launch, and does not yet include observed product usage. Do not build firm personas or pricing decisions from this data alone.

#### Top Themes Ranked By Frequency And Intensity

| Rank | Theme | Frequency | Intensity | Confidence | What it means |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Multi-tool planning friction | 4 of 5 used 2-3 apps or websites | Medium | Medium | The "Planning a UK journey should not take 3 apps" message is worth continuing to test. |
| 2 | Timing and connection anxiety | 3 of 5 mentioned timing, connections, missed connections, or strikes | High | Medium | Journey risk should be explained with concrete examples: missed connections, strikes, long waits, awkward transfers. |
| 3 | Cost sensitivity before booking | 2 of 5 explicitly mentioned price pressure | Medium | Medium | Lead with "real cost before you travel", especially for charities, SMEs, public sector, universities, and budget-conscious teams. |
| 4 | Expense uncertainty and leakage | 2 of 5 had rejected, questioned, or unclaimed expenses | Medium | Medium | Expense confidence is a credible secondary angle, but needs more research with finance/admin buyers. |
| 5 | Receipt and claim admin | 3 of 5 spent 15+ minutes on expenses | Medium | Medium | This may support a future workflow product, but the immediate EndMile wedge should stay pre-travel route comparison. |

#### Jobs To Be Done

**Functional job:** Compare realistic UK business travel options before committing, including first mile, last mile, fares, driving, parking, transfers, disruption risk, and carbon.

**Emotional job:** Feel confident that the route chosen is reasonable, defensible, and not going to create avoidable cost, delays, or expense trouble.

**Social job:** Look like someone who makes sensible travel decisions: cost-aware, practical, reliable, and aligned with company expectations.

#### Best Early Segments To Test Next

| Segment | Why it fits | Risk | Suggested outreach angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consultants and client-facing professionals | Frequent business journeys, often multi-city, easy to understand the pain personally. | They may expense travel without caring about company-wide reporting. | "Would this have helped when planning client-site travel?" |
| University staff and departments | Staff, visitors, conferences, campuses, public-sector cost pressure, sustainability relevance. | Buying process may be slow. | "How do staff and visitors compare rail, driving, parking, and last-mile options?" |
| Event organisers | Repeated venue travel decisions, attendee/staff logistics, awkward last-mile planning. | They may care more about groups than individual business journeys. | "Could this help compare venue travel options before recommending routes?" |
| Business parks and regional offices | Last-mile access is often poor, car vs rail trade-offs matter. | They may be more place-based than journey-planning buyers. | "Could this help people choose practical routes to your site?" |
| Sustainability or ESG leads | Scope 3 travel angle and carbon comparison. | May not own travel behaviour or tools. | "Do route-level carbon comparisons help before travel decisions are made?" |

**Recommended first test:** Start with consultants, university staff, and event organisers. They are easier to reach than corporate travel managers and more likely to understand awkward journey planning immediately.

#### Messaging To Keep Testing

**Primary message:**
```text
EndMile compares the full UK business journey before you travel: cost, time, risk and carbon in one place.
```

**Pain-led message:**
```text
Planning a UK business journey should not mean stitching together Google Maps, Trainline, taxi estimates, parking costs and expense rules yourself.
```

**Discovery question:**
```text
How do you compare train, driving, parking, taxis and last-mile options before booking a work trip today?
```

**Tester CTA:**
```text
Would you try one real journey you have done recently and tell me whether the comparison is useful?
```

#### What Not To Claim Yet
- Do not claim EndMile saves a specific amount of money without usage proof.
- Do not claim the product is a complete Scope 3 reporting system.
- Do not claim corporate travel teams are the confirmed first buyer.
- Do not over-index on carbon until more prospects say that is their urgent problem.
- Do not build personas from the current survey sample alone.

#### Next 30 Conversations Plan

| Segment | Target conversations | Primary question |
|---|---:|---|
| Consultants/client-facing professionals | 10 | "Would this have helped you choose routes when travelling to client sites?" |
| University staff/admin/sustainability | 10 | "How do staff compare travel options for meetings, events, or campus visits?" |
| Event organisers/business parks | 10 | "Do you ever need to recommend or compare travel routes for attendees, visitors, or staff?" |

**Success signal:** At least 5 people try one real route, and at least 3 say the comparison exposes something they would otherwise have checked manually.

**Strongest validation signal:** Someone asks whether EndMile can support repeated journeys, teams, policies, exports, reporting, or shared planning. That means the pain may extend beyond a one-off journey search.

### Emerging Themes

| Theme | Evidence | Confidence | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-app planning is common | 4 of 5 respondents used 2-3 apps or websites for a recent multi-transport work trip. | Medium | EndMile's "3 apps" message is supported by early survey data. |
| Coordination and connections are painful | Respondents mentioned coordinating timings, shortest wait between connections, missed connections, and train strikes. | Medium | Journey risk and connection reliability should stay prominent in messaging. |
| Cost pressure matters | Respondents mentioned reasonable pricing, charity budget constraints, and expensive UK trains. | Medium | Cost comparison should include budget-sensitive framing, not just corporate control. |
| Expense uncertainty creates leakage | 2 of 5 respondents had expenses rejected, questioned, or not claimed because of uncertainty or complexity. | Medium | Pre-travel policy visibility and expense confidence are a credible value angle. |
| Expense admin can be slow | 3 of 5 respondents reported expense claims taking over 30 minutes or 15-30 minutes. | Medium | EndMile should explore workflow value beyond route planning if product scope expands. |

### Strong Voice-of-Customer Quotes
- "Co-ordinating timings and trying to get the shortest wait between connections."
- "Finding the best times to travel to be where I needed to be at a reasonable price."
- "How much trains cost in UK."
- "Wasn't sure, just booked what seemed reasonable."
- "Yes, I decided not to claim something because it seemed too complicated or uncertain."

### Messaging Implications
- Keep using the phrase "before you travel"; respondents show friction before and after the trip, but EndMile's current wedge is pre-travel decision support.
- The strongest early pain is not only booking. It is comparing options under constraints: timings, cost, transfers, disruption, and expense rules.
- Avoid making the product sound like only a carbon reporting tool. Carbon is useful, but survey responses show stronger initial pain around planning time, cost, uncertainty, and expenses.

**Observed Usage Data & Telemetry:**
- **Paul Hardy (Power User / B2B Logistics):** 35 searches across 19 sessions and 8 active days (Aug 28 – Sep 15; including new searches on Tuesday Sep 15: Bristol ➔ Wandsworth London and Reading ➔ Telford). 11 saved journeys with advance scheduling (Sep, Oct, Nov 2026).   - **Inferred Workflow & Buyer Intent:** 
    - Dorset Software Services Ltd employs hundreds of software consultants dispatched from their Poole headquarters to client projects across the UK.
    - Paul's role (`logistics@dorsetsoftware.com`) is coordinating consultant dispatch and approving travel plans.
    - **Why he uses EndMile:** To find the optimal door-to-door cost (driving mileage + parking vs train + station parking + taxi) for each consultant assignment.
    - **The Missing Link (Why PDF / Itinerary Export is Critical):** Paul does not travel himself; he plans the route, but then has to *hand off* the itinerary to the consultant and *justify the cost* to internal finance or client billing. A clean 1-click **Shareable Route Itinerary / PDF Cost Breakdown** solves his entire dispatch and expense-approval workflow without EndMile needing to be a booking engine.
- **Nick Walsh (B2B/Intercity Driver vs Rail):** Registered 2026-08-22, searched Birmingham Moor Street Station $\to$ Milton Keynes (MK5) with `drive: true, train: true`.
- **Jasra Mont (Peak Commuter):** Registered 2026-09-04, morning rush-hour search Potters Bar $\to$ Finsbury Park (London) comparing Train vs Bus.
- **System Performance:** 83/83 app search streams succeeded with HTTP 200 (100% success rate, 0 errors, 0 crashes). Delivered £15.37–£16.54 average savings vs driving and 29.4 kg average CO2 reductions (75–85% less carbon). Total upstream OJP cost across all runs: £2.82.

**Strategic Product & Monetization Takeaway:**
- EndMile's moat is **not** consumer rail split-ticketing (which is dominated by Trainline/SplitMyFare and has high accreditation friction).
- EndMile's true commercial wedge is **Multimodal Total Cost of Ownership (Fuel + Parking vs Rail + Taxi)** for corporate travel planning, plus **B2B 'Plan Your Visit' Venue Widgets (£29–£99/mo)** and **Programmatic SEO arrival guides earning parking/train affiliate revenue**.
