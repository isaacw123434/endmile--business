## LinkedIn Messaging Rules

- Lead with the practical pain: business travel planning currently takes too many apps and too much manual comparison.
- Use fair contrasts against partial tools: Google Maps, Trainline, Citymapper, parking websites, taxi apps, and expense systems.
- Keep the offer grounded in real route decisions: full door-to-door cost, time, journey risk, and carbon before travel.
- Ask for early testers openly.
- Keep carbon beside cost, time, and risk. Do not make the product sound like a generic sustainability tool.
- Avoid overclaiming accuracy, savings, compliance, or buyer validation before proof is collected.
- Usable CTA language: `Try EndMile`, `Looking for early testers`, `Try one real journey`, `Tell me how you make this decision today`.
- For LinkedIn reach, use `Link in comments` in the post body and place the website URL in the first comment.

## Source Data Only

### Chancellor mileage-rate increase

**Status:** Source data and theme only. Do not treat as already published, and do not turn this into final post copy without a fresh prompt.

**Theme:** The 55p mileage rate makes drive-heavy business journeys visibly more expensive. The useful angle is not "all travel got more expensive"; it is that the increase mostly changes routes where the employee drives most of the journey, while rail-led options barely move because most of their cost is fares rather than reimbursed mileage.

**Example route:** Cross Gates, Leeds to Crowne Plaza Newcastle - Stephenson Quarter, departing 10:00 UK time, cycling excluded.

| Option | Time | Cost at 45p | Cost at 55p | CO2e |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Drive only | 1h 47m | GBP 42.07 | GBP 51.41 | 31.78 kg |
| Garforth rail | 2h 00m | GBP 38.47 | GBP 38.91 | 7.49 kg |
| Cross Gates rail | 2h 18m | GBP 36.50 | GBP 36.50 | 7.18 kg |
| Felling Metro P&R | 2h 09m | GBP 45.76 | GBP 54.93 | 31.20 kg |
| Cheapest via York | 2h 47m | GBP 24.90 | GBP 24.90 | 4.88 kg |

**Data note:** Keep rail fares, bus fares, Metro fares, and parking fixed when comparing 45p vs 55p. The point is to isolate the mileage-rate impact.
