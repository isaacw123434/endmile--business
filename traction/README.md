# Traction, Outreach & Engagement Hub

Welcome to EndMile's **Traction Hub**. This is where the founder and the **Business Brain** track real-world commercial momentum, outbound outreach, social content engagement, and the active deal pipeline.

---

## 1. Hub Structure

```text
traction/
├── README.md                     # Operating guide for traction tracking
├── outreach-tracker.md           # Outbound sales touches, channels, follow-ups, and responses
├── social-and-engagement.md      # Social posts (LinkedIn, X), metrics, impressions, comments
└── pipeline.md                   # Active B2B deals, trials, and revenue pipeline
```

---

## 2. How the Brain & Founder Collaborate

Whenever you:
1. **Send outbound emails or LinkedIn messages**: Tell the Brain (e.g. *"I messaged 15 theatres in Yorkshire today using Angle A"*). The Brain logs it in [`outreach-tracker.md`](outreach-tracker.md), sets follow-up dates, and monitors response rates.
2. **Publish a post or update**: Tell the Brain (e.g. *"I just posted this breakdown on LinkedIn: [link/text]"*). The Brain logs the post, tracks performance, analyzes comments, and plans follow-up content in [`social-and-engagement.md`](social-and-engagement.md).
3. **Receive a reply, objection, or demo request**: Tell the Brain. The Brain drafts your rebuttal or demo follow-up, logs customer feedback in [`.agents/customer-feedback.md`](../.agents/customer-feedback.md), and moves the prospect through [`pipeline.md`](pipeline.md).
