## 2. Voice & Messaging Principles

- **Contrast-Driven**: Always pit EndMile against the flawed status quo (bouncing across Google Maps, Trainline, RAC, parking apps).
- **British English & Concrete Math**: Use exact figures (£110 mileage, 55p AMAP, £2,250 G-Cloud fee, -61% CO₂), never vague corporate slogans.
- **Door-to-Door Truth**: Emphasize that travel decisions require first-mile, last-mile, parking, and station transfer visibility.
