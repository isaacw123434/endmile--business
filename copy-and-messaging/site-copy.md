## Section 5: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q: How does EndMile calculate true door-to-door costs?
A: EndMile integrates official National Rail OJP timetable and fare APIs, OpenStreetMap routing engines, regional UK parking tariff databases, and HMRC statutory Approved Mileage Allowance Payments (55p/25p AMAP). We compute the full sum: driving mileage + fuel/VAT + station parking + rail fare + local transit/taxi legs.

#### Q: Do my consultants or attendees need to create an account?
A: No. Consultants receive a lightweight, zero-login mobile web link with calling points, SatNav station postcodes, and one-tap calendar export.

#### Q: How does the venue widget compare to You. Smart. Thing. (YST)?
A: YST targets five-figure enterprise transport authority tenders with £2,250 minimum setup fees and heavy iframe embeds. EndMile is built for the 99% unserved mid-market: £0 setup fee, £19–£49/month, lightweight fluid mobile drawers, and automated Arts Council England green reporting.
