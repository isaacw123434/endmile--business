## Strategic Operating Rules for AI Agents

1. **Never Compete Head-on with Legacy TMCs or Enterprise MaaS on Booking:**
   - Navan, SAP Concur, Perk, and Mobilleo have raised hundreds of millions to build global flight/hotel inventory booking engines.
   - EndMile's wedge is **pre-trip decision support, margin protection, and client invoice justification** (proving total cost of ownership before booking/travel occurs).

2. **The YST Wedge (The Mid-Market Advantage):**
   - Do NOT bid on £50k+ transport authority tenders (TfGM / Bee Network).
   - Attack the **4,992 UK cultural institutions and venues** that cannot justify YST's £2,250 setup fee and £750/day consultancy rate.
   - Offer £0 setup, self-serve embed under 2 minutes, and £19–£49/mo pricing.
