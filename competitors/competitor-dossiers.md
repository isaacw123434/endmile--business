## 2. Deep Differentiators for EndMile

### A. The "Total Cost of Ownership" (TCO) vs Just Speed
Competitors show either:
1. Pure travel time (Google Maps, Citymapper).
2. Pure ticket fare (Trainline).
3. Post-trip expense claims (Concur, Navan).

EndMile is the **only UK platform calculating True Door-to-Door TCO before travel occurs**:
- Direct drive: Fuel + Congestion/Clean Air Zones (CAZ) + Station/City Parking + Wear & Tear (HMRC 55p AMAP).
- Rail: Exact Train Fares + Station Parking + First/Last Mile Bus/Taxi transfers.
- Opportunity cost: Productive train time vs lost driving time.

### B. Consultant Travel Reimbursement & Invoice Defense
- UK IT and professional services consultancies lose **1% to 5% of EBTA** to client invoice travel disputes.
- A client rejects a £110 mileage claim (200 miles @ HMRC 55p) citing a £60 Trainline ticket.
- EndMile's **Pre-Trip Travel Cost Justification PDF** provides the irrefutable evidence proving that Train (£60) + Station Parking (£15) + Destination Taxi (£25) = £100, saving £10 in direct cost while preserving 2 productive billable hours.

### C. Cultural Venue Travel Widget Accessibility
- Independent museums and theatres cannot afford YST's £2,250 G-Cloud onboarding fee.
- EndMile offers:
  - 1-minute copy-paste HTML embed snippet.
  - £0 setup fee.
  - 100% fluid mobile layout (no nested iframe scrollbars).
  - Scope 3 Category 6 carbon reporting compliant with Arts Council England (ACE) Julie's Bicycle requirements.
