# EndMile Consultancy App Outbound Outreach Master Plan & Template Bank

**Author:** Isaac Willoughby & EndMile Business Brain  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Objective:** Acquire the first 10–25 paying B2B consultancy users (£19/mo Pro Planner) for the EndMile App, focusing exclusively on the Paul Hardy profile (UK IT, software, engineering, and management consultancies).

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Pivot

1. **Focus Exclusively on the App Wedge:** Venue widget outreach is paused. 100% of outbound bandwidth is dedicated to UK consultancies and field service firms (the Paul Hardy profile).
2. **Dorset Software Services Removed:** Dorset Software (`APP-001`) is marked as `Excluded - Existing User (Paul Hardy)` across both `app_prospects_v1.csv` and `endmile app prospects v1.xlsx` and hard-coded into the CLI exclusion list.
3. **No Competitor Counter-Pitching:** We do not mention or fight competitors (like YST) in outreach. We focus purely on EndMile's own value: saving 10–15 minutes per route, comparing HMRC 55p mileage against rail TCO in 10 seconds, and eliminating multi-tab headaches.
4. **Humanized Pacing (10–20 Mins Apart):** The automated dispatcher enforces a 10 to 20-minute randomized pause between sends during UK business hours (08:30–17:30 Mon–Fri), ensuring deliverability looks 100% human.
5. **The Pre-Revenue Founder Strategy:** Because we are pre-revenue, we A/B test a humble, authentic "independent UK software engineer looking for feedback" approach alongside the "helpful tool utility" hook.

---

## 2. Strategic Angle: Pre-Revenue Founder vs Product Utility

### Why the Pre-Revenue Founder Angle Wins in the UK
* British B2B professionals and gatekeepers have strong defensive reflexes against sales pitches and aggressive SaaS vendors.
* When a software vendor emails saying *"We built a platform, would you like a demo?"*, receptionists hit delete.
* However, when an **independent UK software engineer and founder** politely asks for 2 quick questions or feedback while building a tool, receptionists forward it to the operations manager, and operations leads often say yes to support an independent builder.

### The 3-Way A/B Testing Matrix (Automatically Rotated on `info@` Inboxes)

```
[info@ Triage Inbox]
       │
       ├── Variant A (Founder Discovery Ask - 38 words)
       │     └─ "Independent UK engineer building a tool... 2 quick questions on how you currently handle it"
       │
       ├── Variant B (Helpful Tool Utility - 50 words)
       │     └─ "Put together a simple tool that works out driving vs train in 10s... worth passing to travel team?"
       │
       └── Variant C (HMRC 55p Mileage Dispute - 45 words)
             └─ "Tool to back up HMRC 55p mileage against rail when clients dispute travel invoices"
```

## 4. Operational Plan & Execution Guardrails

| Parameter | Guardrail / Rule | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily Send Limit** | **5 emails per day** (Max 10) | Preserves `endmilerouting.co.uk` reputation; stays well under bulk spam thresholds. |
| **Send Window** | **08:30 – 17:30 UK Time, Mon–Fri** | Gatekeepers and office managers read and forward emails during active morning/afternoon hours. |
| **Pacing Delay** | **10 to 20 minutes (600–1200s) random pause** | Completely mimics a human sending an email between normal tasks. |
| **Data Synchronization** | Automated across CSV, Excel, and `outreach-tracker.md` | Zero manual spreadsheet management for the founder. |

### Commands:
```bash
# 1. Preview the next 5 generated emails (Dry Run):
npm run outreach:preview

# 2. Live Send 5 emails during the business day:
npm run outreach:send
```
