# Founder Drop Zone & Strategic Advisory Archive

This directory serves as the fast-capture drop zone and strategic archive for Isaac and the **EndMile Business Brain**.

---

## 1. Directory Structure

```text
notes/
├── README.md               # This operating guide
├── inbox/                  # Quick scratchpad, voice transcripts, uncurated brain-dumps
├── meetings/               # Structured prospect, client, and partner meeting logs
├── advisory/               # Strategic memos, risk analyses, and reviews authored by the Brain
└── templates/              # Reusable markdown templates
    ├── meeting-note-template.md
    ├── idea-or-hypothesis-template.md
    ├── objection-log-template.md
    └── advisory-memo-template.md
```

---

## 2. Ingestion & Memory Protocol

- Whenever you have a raw thought, messy call notes, or an idea, drop it into [`notes/inbox/`](inbox/) (or paste directly in chat).
- The Brain extracts:
  - Outreach targets $\rightarrow$ [`traction/outreach-tracker.md`](../traction/outreach-tracker.md)
  - Post ideas / feedback $\rightarrow$ [`traction/social-and-engagement.md`](../traction/social-and-engagement.md)
  - Copy ideas $\rightarrow$ [`copy-and-messaging/`](../copy-and-messaging/)
  - Customer quotes $\rightarrow$ [`.agents/customer-feedback.md`](../.agents/customer-feedback.md)
  - Strategic decisions $\rightarrow$ [`.agents/decisions.md`](../.agents/decisions.md)
