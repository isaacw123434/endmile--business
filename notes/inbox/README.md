# Notes Inbox (Zero-Friction Capture Zone)

This folder is your fast-capture drop zone.

## How To Use
1. Whenever you have a raw thought, meeting transcript, voice note, customer objection, or idea, create a quick `.md` or `.txt` file here (e.g. `2026-09-25-call-with-theatre-director.md` or `quick-idea-tfl-fares.md`).
2. You can also simply paste your notes directly into your chat with your **Business Notes Advisor**.
3. During sessions, your Advisor will:
   - Read the raw notes in `inbox/`.
   - Distill the key insights, quotes, and decisions.
   - Route customer quotes to [`.agents/customer-feedback.md`](../../.agents/customer-feedback.md).
   - Route decisions to [`.agents/decisions.md`](../../.agents/decisions.md).
   - Move or archive the processed note to `notes/meetings/` or `notes/advisory/`.
